Institute of Acoustics, Chinese Academy of Sciences · with Dr. Peng Yong

Since October 2025. I work on 3-D ultrasonic phased-array imaging of rock blocks from full-matrix capture data (16-element transmit/receive arrays), using IQ demodulation and delay-and-sum beamforming, with Kirchhoff migration as a comparison.
